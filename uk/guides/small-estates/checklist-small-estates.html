What to do
Register the death and get the death certificate
Needed regardless of the estate's size.
Contact each institution directly
Ask what they specifically need to release funds without a full grant.
Tip: England & Wales/NI have no single nationwide figure – each bank sets its own limit.
Bring proof of your relationship
As executor, administrator, or next of kin, alongside the death certificate.
Apply for a full grant if needed
If the balance exceeds the institution's limit – see our Grant of Probate guide.
