The first two weeks: a checklist

Free checklist · nachklang.app/uk/guides/first-two-weeks/

The first few hours
Give yourself a moment
You don't have to sort everything out at once. Nothing is legally due in the first hours.
Let the people close to you know
Immediate family, close friends, the person's employer – a call means more than a text.
Contact a doctor
Issues the medical certificate of cause of death – needed before anything else can proceed.
The first few days
Arrange a funeral director
Most are reachable around the clock and can guide you through the local process.
Register the death
Within 5 days in England & Wales (8 in Scotland) – order at least 5–8 certified copies.
Tip: banks, insurers and pension providers may each want their own original.
Use Tell Us Once
One report covers HMRC, the DWP, DVLA, the council and the Passport Office. 28 days to use it.
Look for a will
Check papers or ask their solicitor. No will? The rules of intestacy apply.
Secure the home and the post
Check post regularly, keep keys safe, leave utilities connected for now.
