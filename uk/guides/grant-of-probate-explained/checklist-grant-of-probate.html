Grant of Probate and Confirmation, explained

Free checklist · nachklang.app/uk/guides/grant-of-probate-explained/

England & Wales / Northern Ireland
Work out who applies
Named executor (will) applies for Probate; closest relative (no will) applies for Letters of Administration.
Value the estate
Rough valuation of assets and debts, and check whether Inheritance Tax is due.
Apply to the Probate Registry
Online or on paper, with the death certificate, the will (if any), and the valuation.
Tip: online applications are usually processed faster than paper ones.
Scotland
Prepare the C1 inventory
Lists and values every asset – part of the Confirmation application itself.
Apply to the Sheriff Court
For the area where the person lived. Small estates: the sheriff clerk can help prepare it.
Once you have it
Present it to banks, insurers, the Land Registry
Your proof of authority to deal with the estate – not the death certificate.
Nachklang helps with
Tracking which institutions need proof of authority
And generating the letters once you have your Grant or Confirmation in hand.
